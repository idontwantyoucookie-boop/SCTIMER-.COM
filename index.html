How to read a scramble

Hold the cube with white on top and green facing you, then do the moves in order. The picture next to the scramble (3x3 only) shows how your cube should look.

R right, L left, U up, D down, F front, B back. A letter alone is a quarter turn clockwise as you look at that face. R' is counterclockwise and R2 is a half turn.
